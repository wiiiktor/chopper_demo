Chopper training curves

The choppers learn which tokens of 16 retrieved passages to keep; the Qwen3-1.7B answerer learns to answer or ask a follow-up question. The two mmBERTs and the MiniCPM3-4B slice (MLA, multi-head latent attention: every slice head reads the full model's whole conversation cache) are trained once on all 54k rows mixed (benchmark + Wikipedia). The solid Qwen slice saw 9,000 benchmark rows, then the other 18,000, then Wikipedia rows after 27k; dashed lines: the Qwen slice on all 54k mixed (stopped at 18k) and the MiniCPM3 20% slice (8 heads + 960 neurons per layer; the 10% run stopped at 4.5k for it). Train lines are 500-row rolling means; validation points are the mean over MuSiQue, HotpotQA and TriviaQA (300 questions), read at p > 0.5.
