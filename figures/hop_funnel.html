Where multi-hop answers get lost

100 MuSiQue questions per hop count, each with its own 20 paragraphs, so every needed paragraph is always there and nothing is lost to search. Read each diagram like website traffic: questions flow left to right, and at every hop the ones whose paragraph the chopper (or teacher) did not keep drop out.

Questions

Hop k kept: some kept text lies inside the paragraph that states hop k's fact. Answer in notes: the final answer string itself is in the kept text. A question can keep a piece of every needed paragraph and still miss the answer, when the kept piece of the last paragraph is not the sentence with the answer; those drop out as "answer cut".
