3. Long-conversation memory

On LoCoMo and LongMemEval-s, plain RAG over the raw history beats the chopper, and even the gold evidence alone tops out near .58. The 4B answerer is the main limit: it never says "not mentioned", rarely states preferences, and copies relative dates instead of resolving them.
