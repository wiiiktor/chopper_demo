Chopper: with and without

A question that needs two facts from 20 retrieved Wikipedia paragraphs. Without Chopper, Qwen3-14B reads all 20 paragraphs. With Chopper, it reads only the exact quotes Chopper cut out, about 12× fewer tokens. Same model, same prompt.
